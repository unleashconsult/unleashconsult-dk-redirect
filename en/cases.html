Client cases

Selected engagements, told from the situation the client was in.

Restarting an IT project

Abena Data

2026 · Project Manager and Application Owner

Abena Data delivers IT for Abena's global e-commerce platform, where further development and daily operations run side by side. I stepped into a running project with four teams and an external development partner working in parallel tracks.

The first step was to clarify deliverables, acceptance criteria, ownership of each deliverable and budget with IT management and the business. I then brought the tracks together in one plan and one shared status picture for teams and management. New vendor tasks were created in Abena's own tool, so prioritisation stayed with Abena, and decisions were confirmed in writing after every meeting.

Abena Data's part of an international market launch was delivered a few days ahead of schedule, with one plan that both teams and management steered by.

Platform replacement

B Company ApS

2019 – 2021 · Project Manager

B Company's webshop ran on PrestaShop and had to move to Magento with close integration to ERP. The company had no PIM system.

I gathered requirements across finance, IT, development and marketing and wrote the requirements specification. I briefed the vendors and prepared decision papers for management on vendor and tech stack. I then led the implementation with an external development partner: Magento, the ERP integration and a new PIM, with ongoing budget follow-up.

The webshop went live on the new platform and was handed over to internal operations. Online revenue quadrupled over the period.

Konges Sløjd A/S

2021 – 2022 · E-commerce project lead

Konges Sløjd sold from a smaller Shopify store and was expanding into new markets.

I selected and managed the development agency that redesigned the stores, and upgraded the platform from Shopify to Shopify Plus with full integration to the existing ERP and finance system. Content for the new markets was localised through an external translation agency, and management received decision papers on vendors and tech stack.

Eight localised webshops went live in new markets on Shopify Plus, integrated with the existing ERP and finance system. Each new market built on the previous one instead of starting from scratch.

Implementing a new system

Plytix

2023 – 2025 · Project Manager

Plytix, a SaaS company, had chosen the attribution tool Dreamdata for use across the whole commercial organisation.

I led the implementation with API integrations between the CRM and other data sources and coordinated between Business Operations, development, sales, management and the vendor. I mapped what each team needed the tool for, set up data governance and wrote both technical documentation and a user guide. Marketing got workshops built on examples from their own daily work.

The tool became part of the commercial organisation's daily work, with one shared KPI framework and documentation that explained both the numbers and their limits.
